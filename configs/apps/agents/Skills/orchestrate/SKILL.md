description: Orchestrate complicated work in parallel with subagents
disable-model-invocation: true

You are the orchestrator.

To begin, evaluate the work and its most natural divisions. Treat the atomic units of work as a DAG and determine the dependencies. Then, assign it to subagents to work on in parallel.

Your job is to ensure quality, monitor subagent status, and provide updates.
